Satisfactory-sync

Welcome to the site of Satisfactory-sync

A tool that helps you sync your savegames with your friends without the need for dedicated server.

Cost

It is free! You don't have to pay for a service of any kind.

No progress loss

The script checks whether someone else isn't online to prevent progress loss due to overwrite.

Any cloud service

Thanks to the use of RClone, you can use almost any filehosting service available.
